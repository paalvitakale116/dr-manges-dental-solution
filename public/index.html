Common questions

Is a root canal painful?

The treatment is done under local anaesthesia, so most patients feel little during the procedure. Mild soreness afterwards usually settles in a few days.

How often should I get a check-up?

Many people visit every six months. Dr. Mange will suggest a schedule that suits your teeth and gums.

How long do whitening results last?

It depends on habits such as tea, coffee and smoking. Regular cleaning helps the result last longer.

Do I need an appointment?

An appointment is recommended so you are not kept waiting. Use the form below to request a slot.

Can I get a treatment plan before I decide?

Yes. After an examination, Dr. Mange explains the options so you can decide with full information.
